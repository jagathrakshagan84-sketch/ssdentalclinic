OUR PRACTICE

About SS Dental Care

Learn about our clinic philosophy, hygiene standards, and meet Dr. Gowtham, BDS.

A Premium Local Dental Clinic

SS Dental Care was established with a singular objective: to deliver professional, modern, and accessible dental healthcare to the residents of Ammapettai and surrounding regions.

We understand that visiting a dentist can sometimes cause anxiety. That is why our clinic has been meticulously designed to feel warm, comforting, and welcoming. We place a high emphasis on hygiene, implementing rigorous, multi-stage clinical sterilization processes for every instrument and surface.

From routine oral cleanings to complex restorative solutions, our treatments are tailored carefully to meet each patient's unique diagnostic needs, without unnecessary complications.

CLINICAL DIRECTORY

Meet Dr. Gowtham, BDS

Dr. Gowtham, BDS is the chief dental surgeon and founder of SS Dental Care. He holds a Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) degree and specializes in modern family dentistry, preventive dental care, and pain management procedures.

Dr. Gowtham believes in patient-centered dentistry. He takes the time to explain clinical findings to patients, outline proposed treatment paths in simple terms, and answer any questions before beginning treatment. His gentle clinical touch has helped many patients overcome their fear of root canals and dental extractions.

Core Values:

  • ✔ Clear, honest communication regarding treatments.
  • ✔ Zero shortcuts in hygiene, disinfection, and safety.
  • ✔ Comfort-first local anesthetic and pain management techniques.
